# About

I'm Martin Musale, a backend and platform software engineer based in Nairobi, Kenya. I consult at [Acoruss](https://acoruss.com), where I head the technology wing.

I work mostly with Go, Python, and TypeScript. My work has covered APIs and SDKs as well as the infrastructure that runs services.

## Engineering background

At Microsoft, I worked in the M365 Core team on the platform behind Microsoft 365 products, including SharePoint, Office, and Copilot. I built and managed services that helped people get relevant signals and content in their Copilot experiences.

I also worked on a cross-cloud platform built on [Azure Kubernetes Service](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/aks/what-is-aks). It manages containers in Azure and other clouds so developers can run applications without provisioning the underlying compute themselves.

Before that, I worked in Graph Developer Experiences. I contributed to [Kiota](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/openapi/kiota/overview), which generates SDKs from OpenAPI specifications, and helped release and maintain [Microsoft Graph SDKs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/sdks/sdks-overview) for Python, Go, TypeScript, Java, and C#.

I worked on the initial SDKs for Copilot endpoints in Python, TypeScript, and C#, and developed [Semantic Kernel](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/semantic-kernel/overview/) scenarios that used Microsoft Graph endpoints, including files. I also helped deliver an updated version of [Microsoft Graph Toolkit](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/toolkit/overview), with improvements to its web components, performance, and integration.

My [CV]({{< relref "cv/_index.md" >}}) has the fuller work history. [Projects]({{< relref "projects/_index.md" >}}) covers some of my own experiments and open-source work.
